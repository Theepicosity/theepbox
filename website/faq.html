Frequently Asked Questions

What does theepbox add? See: patch notes.
Is theepbox compatible with Ultrabox? At the moment, I haven't tried too much with compatability. You are welcome to try it out for yourself, and report the reults!
Does theepbox have an offline version? Yes. You can access it here here.
You can either download an OS-specific version that has custom sampling support, or the generic HTML version that lacks custom sampling support.
The offline version tends to update for every other online update
Does theepbox claim ownership over songs created with it? theepbox does not claim ownership over songs created with it, so original songs belong to their authors.

Neither theepbox nor its creators assume responsibility for any copyrighted material played on theepbox. No songs are ever received, recorded, or distributed by theepbox's servers. All song data is contained in the URL after the hash (#) mark, and theepbox running inside your browser converts that data into sound waves.
When will x feature be added? Feel free to request features with the links below! I can't guarantee that I will get to them all the time but I certainly will keep them in mind.
Where can I contact the developers? You can contact the developers via the BeepBox Modding Discord.
You can also contact us on the issues page if you have ideas/suggestions or have encountered any bugs.
